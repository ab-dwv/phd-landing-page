# Sharda University — Ph.D. admissions hero

A responsive first-viewport concept in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No runtime dependencies.

Run `npm run dev` and open http://127.0.0.1:4173. Run `npm run build` for deployable files in `dist/`.

## Enquiry behavior

The form validates the details and explicit contact consent, then prepares an email to Sharda's published Ph.D. admissions address. The applicant must choose **Continue by email** and send it in their own email client. The page never claims a lead was submitted, stores no personal data, and makes no tracking requests. Connect an approved CRM endpoint and Google Ads conversion tracking before a paid campaign launch. Fire conversion events only after confirmed lead acceptance.

## Content and assets

Research figures (1,300+ scholars; 9,315+ papers; 150+ major funded research projects), full-time/part-time modes, logo, and photography come from https://admissions.sharda.ac.in/phd/ and https://www.sharda.ac.in/. Verified 14 November 2026. The published July 2026 intake is closed; this design makes no claim about a new intake, deadline or admissions opening. Research interests are broad enquiry categories, not a guarantee of available seats.
